## Remaining boundary

A separately authorized review must establish whether backend/library consumers can observe or depend on this topology and define a defensible equivalence boundary before another variant or additional prototype execution. This task does not approve narrowing that contract. Even with that resolved, prototype vector/alias/interop/error and eight-table behavior still require focused proof; nine unknown files, runtime/upstream provenance and full dependency closure remain open.
